Blink Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Blink is a planning assistant that turns your goals into a schedule and helps you keep it. This page explains what Blink stores, why, and what control you have over it.

What Blink stores

  • Your plans. Commitments, tasks, scheduled sessions, milestones, no-touch zones, and the notes you teach Blink (for example, your work hours or a standing gym time). These exist so Blink can plan around your real life.
  • Your recent conversation. A short rolling window of your latest exchanges with Blink, kept so it remembers what you were just talking about. Older messages are dropped automatically.
  • Measured focus time. Minutes recorded by the focus timer and outcomes you report at check-in.
  • Your name and email, only if you sign in with Google. Used to greet you and to keep your plans attached to your account. Blink never asks for a password; sign-in happens on Google's own screen.

Google Calendar

If you grant calendar access, Blink reads your busy times so it never schedules over them, and it can add reminder events. Blink only writes to your calendar after you explicitly confirm each write in the app. You can revoke this access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

Where your data lives

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, encrypted at rest, in a project accessible only to Blink's runtime service account. Your conversations with Blink are processed by Google's Gemini models on Vertex AI to generate replies and plans.

What Blink does not do

  • No selling of your data, and no advertising.
  • No sharing with third parties beyond the Google Cloud services that run the app.
  • No reading or writing of anything in your Google account beyond the calendar access you explicitly grant.

Your choices

  • You can use Blink without signing in at all. A guest workspace stays on your browser.
  • You can sign out at any time in Settings.
  • You can revoke calendar and account access from your Google Account permissions page.
  • To have your stored data deleted, email us and we will remove your workspace.
